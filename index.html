SETTLE IN

Accommodation corner.

Useful conversations and reminders for students looking for a place to stay around Abraka.

01

Do your checks

Inspect a property in person where possible. Confirm the address, facilities, terms and total costs before paying.

02

Ask questions

Speak with trusted students and people familiar with the area. Do not rely on an online listing alone.

03

Stay safe

Avoid sending deposits to unverified people. Keep receipts and written records of any agreement.

No rooms or landlords are verified or endorsed by this website at this time.
